# Individual Technical Reflection (complete after implementation)

Address the following in your own words:
1. Why was a relational model selected for reservation, room allocation and payment data?
2. What design changes were required when adapting an Airbnb-style model to a hotel system?
3. Which query was most expensive before optimisation, and what changed after indexing?
4. What concurrency problem did you test, and how did locking/isolation address it?
5. What information was modelled in MongoDB, and why was it suitable for a document model?
6. What are the limitations of asynchronous replication and the proposed sharding approach?
7. What failed during implementation, how did you diagnose it, and what did you learn?

Use only challenges and test results you actually experienced.
